Automation

Work that runs on its own, and lets itself be checked

Flows that take repetitive work out of the way, and agents that prepare the work of judgement without deciding it. Where a signature is needed, a person stays.

01

The mail first, then the reply

First thing in the morning: which emails need an answer today, and why. The order is by urgency, not by time of arrival.

02

The draft, with the customer’s data

Second thing: replying. The agent reads the customer record, the orders and the terms already agreed, and writes a draft that invents nothing.

03

The flows I keep in service

Real business cases, each one tested before it is delivered. The value is not the flow: it is knowing which of the two ways is needed.

#FlowWhat it does
01 Enquiries from the site It checks the data, scores it on five weighted dimensions, gives it a priority and routes it with different response times. At the end it explains why it gave that score.
02 Supplier invoices It checks the VAT number, the consistency between taxable amount and tax, duplicates and the gap from the historical median. It stops the ones that do not add up.
03 Currency exchange It reads the official rates and gives notice only when the change goes past half a point. No pointless notifications.
04 Support requests It classifies, assigns and routes. What it cannot classify it puts in a queue for a person, it does not guess.
05 Documents It sorts and files. It exists in two versions: one with the model, one without. The second is faster and always fails at the same point: it is fixed once and for all.
06 Content It prepares carousels, stories and newsletters with a preview, and waits for confirmation before publishing.

04

With the model, or without

The question I ask first. A deterministic flow costs less, is fixed in a minute and always fails at the same point: where it is enough, I use that.

Without a modelWhen the rule can be written down
With the modelWhen the text changes every time
The skillKnowing which of the two is needed

The rule

What is repetitive and verifiable gets automated. Where judgement is needed, a person stays.

Stated limit

An automation nobody watches becomes a silent failure. Every flow I deliver has a log, an alert when it breaks and a way to stop it: without those I do not deliver it.
