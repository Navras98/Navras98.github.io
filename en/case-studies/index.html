Case studies

From a single agent to a team

Three shapes I build and keep in service: an agent that prepares and publishes content, an agent inside an application, a group of agents that split the work and check each other.

01

Content agent

It proposes, waits, publishes. The proposals come from the material of the week, and each one states the source it comes from.

Make me a carousel of 5 posts for Instagram
2 tool calls 9 s
Three proposals from the material of the week. Each one is a carousel of five panels:
TOPICTHREAD OF THE CAROUSELSOURCE
AI newsWhat came out this week and what changes for people at work4 articles, 21 August
FinancePrice per token against cost per task: the real arithmeticTest bench, 21 August
SecurityFive levels, and why the fifth is not a guaranteeYour note of 12 August
Go with the second one, drier tone
1 tool call 6 s
Five panels written: opening with the number, two of comparison, one with the sum worked out, closing with the question. Alt text included.
AWAITING APPROVAL · I publish only after your yes

The creative part stays with the person who signs the profile. The agent’s job is to prepare, to order and to wait.

02

Agents inside an application

Not a chat bolted on the side: an agent that sits inside the product and does one precise thing, with the permissions of the user who called it and not with its own.

BoundaryThe permissions are those of whoever asks
OperationsDeclared in the code, not improvised
FallbackIf it cannot, it says so and stops
Prepare the summary of the month for tomorrow’s meeting
executor · 6 tool calls 38 s
Summary ready: receipts, open invoices, three late orders. Every number with the read that produces it.
verifier (read only) · traceability check 12 s
REJECTED · one value does not trace back to any read
Corrected: the estimated margin was removed and declared missing. The rest passed the check.
approved · 14 values, 14 proofs 3 s

The verifier has read-only permissions and no interest in declaring the work finished.

03

Team of agents

One executes, one verifies. The verifier checks that every number traces back to a read: when it does not, it rejects, and the work goes back.

The shape that matters

The one who executes is not the one who verifies.

Stated limit

A team of agents is not right for everything: it adds steps, time and cost. I use it where a silent error costs more than the extra complexity, and I say so when it is not the case.
