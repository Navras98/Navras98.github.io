Who does it, how many times a week, what information they have in front of them while they do it, and what they do when a piece of information is missing. Four lines like these say more than a page of requirements.

Four lines about a process that already exists

There is no need for a specification, nor even for an idea of which technology to use. If you have already tried to solve it somehow, tell me: knowing where an attempt stopped shortens the work more than any other piece of information.

02

I reply with the questions that remain

They are almost always about the data: where it sits, in what form, who can see it and whether it can leave the company. They come before everything else, because the whole architecture depends on the answer.

DataWhere it sits and in what form
AccessWho can see it
BoundariesWhether it can leave the company

Stated limit

I do not work on many projects at once: the part that takes attention is not writing the system, it is understanding the process of the people who will use it. A request usually finds room within a few weeks.
